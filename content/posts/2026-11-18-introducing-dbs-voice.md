Missed calls are missed business. DBS Voice is an AI receptionist built by the group to make sure every call gets answered, whether it comes in at 9am or 9pm.

## What it does

- **Answers every call**, around the clock.
- **Takes messages and qualifies leads**, so you know who called and what they need.
- **Books appointments** directly while the caller is on the line.
- **Transfers to a person** when a caller needs to speak to your team.

## Keep your existing number

There's no need to change your phone number. You simply divert calls to DBS Voice, and it picks up from there.
