InvoiceFlow: no more retyping supplier invoices

Our cloud invoice automation captures supplier invoices, extracts the data with OCR and posts it into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage.

Manually keying in supplier invoices is slow and error-prone. InvoiceFlow is the group's cloud platform for taking that work off your team.

How it works

  1. Capture. Upload or forward supplier invoices.
  2. Extract. OCR reads the supplier, dates, line totals and VAT.
  3. Post. The data goes straight into your accounting system.

Built for accounting firms

InvoiceFlow handles many clients from one account and works with Xero, QuickBooks and Sage, so firms can process invoices for their whole client base in one place.

Want to see it on your own invoices? Book a demo or open InvoiceFlow.
